KILLING DUCK

Kills You, Kills Brain Rot

BEFORE THE DUCK WAKES UP

To do its job, the duck needs to look at your browsing. This is everything it reads and keeps.

What it reads

  • Your open tabs: how many there are, and their titles and web addresses. It uses them to count tabs, to list them in the popup so you can close them, and to spot sites on your brain rot list.
  • Whether you are at the computer: Chrome tells it if the keyboard or mouse was used in the last 2 minutes, so only active time counts. It never sees what you type or click.
  • Web pages: it draws its warnings on top of them. During a focus session it pauses videos on brain rot sites. It does not read what is on your pages.

What it keeps

  • For each of the last 60 days: active browsing minutes, minutes on brain rot sites, tabs killed and focus sessions. Plus your XP, streak and settings.
  • It keeps no list of the sites you visit.

Where it goes

  • Nowhere. Everything stays in Chrome's storage on this computer. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no ads.
  • If you install the optional Windows tab strip companion, the titles of the tabs in your focused window are passed to that program on this computer, so the duck can find the tab to peck.
  • Removing the extension deletes all of it.

Until you agree, the duck stays off and tracks nothing.

Changed your mind later? Switch the duck off in its popup, or remove it on chrome://extensions.
