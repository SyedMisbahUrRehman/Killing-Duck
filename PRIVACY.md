# Killing Duck privacy policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Killing Duck is a Chrome extension that helps you cut down on tab overload
and mindless browsing. It does not collect, sell or share your data. Nothing
it reads or keeps ever leaves your computer.

## What the extension reads

- **Your open tabs:** how many there are, and their titles and web
  addresses. It uses them to count tabs, to list them in the popup so you
  can close them, and to spot sites on your brain rot list.
- **Whether you are at the computer:** Chrome's idle API reports whether
  the keyboard or mouse was used in the last 2 minutes, so only active
  browsing time is counted. The extension never sees what you type or click.
- **Web pages:** a content script draws the duck's warnings on top of
  pages. During a focus session it pauses videos on brain rot sites. It does
  not read or record what is on your pages.

## What the extension keeps

In Chrome's local storage on your computer:

- for each of the last 60 days: active browsing minutes, minutes on brain
  rot sites, tabs closed and focus sessions;
- your XP, level, streak and settings, including the brain rot site list
  you edit.

It keeps no list of the sites you visit.

## What leaves your computer

Nothing. Killing Duck has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no
third-party code. It does not use Chrome sync storage.

If you install the optional Windows tab strip companion, the extension passes
the titles of the tabs in your focused Chrome window to that program, which
runs on the same computer, so the duck can find the tab it pecks. The
companion keeps none of them; its log file records only start-ups and errors.

## Your choices

- Nothing is tracked until you agree to this on the extension's welcome page.
- The switch in the popup turns the duck off. While it is off, nothing is
  tracked.
- Removing the extension deletes everything it stored.

## Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

The use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web
Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Changes

If what the extension reads or keeps changes, the extension will tell you
before the change takes effect, and this page will be updated.
